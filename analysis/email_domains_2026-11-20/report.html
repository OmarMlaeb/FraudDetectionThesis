Findings

Risk depends strongly on whether a domain is the purchaser or recipient field. Some training-selected domains retain elevated fraud rates in validation and test, while others have very small held-out groups and no observed test fraud. The full counts below distinguish high fraud volume from high fraud rate.

Among the 8,208 test transactions in the training-selected recipient domains, 1,122 are fraudulent. Only 49 transactions have an email-graph neighbour, and 0 of those are frauds. Thus 1,122 frauds in this group are isolated in the email graph. Large selected domains are excluded by the group-size limit.

MLP has higher mean test PR-AUC and F1 than email-GraphSAGE in 4 of the four pooled inside/outside populations. Email-GraphSAGE versus main-GraphSAGE varies by field and domain. These observations do not support a general explanation that linking high-risk email domains makes email-GraphSAGE superior. The individual-domain tables preserve exceptions and threshold-dependent differences.

Scope and interpretation

The risk screen was fixed before examining held-out rates: at least 100 training transactions, training fraud rate at least twice the overall training rate, and its marginal 95% Wilson lower bound above that rate. This is an exploratory screen, not a multiplicity-corrected significance test. Selected domains are frozen for validation/test; no domains are added using future labels. Wilson intervals assume binomial observations and do not adjust for repeated-entity dependence. Persistence below is descriptive relative to each split baseline.
